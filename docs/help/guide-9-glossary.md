# Glossary

This glossary explains the terms used in the guide, in the sense the system uses them. Terms are in alphabetical order.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Account group | A family of ledger accounts under one of the five categories (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses), for example Bank or Accounts Receivable. |
| AED | UAE dirham. The base currency: every ledger amount is held in it. |
| Ageing | A list of what is owed grouped by how late it is: not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and over 90 days past the due date. |
| Approval | The step that turns a draft document into an Approved one. Only approval moves stock, posts the ledger, updates the party's balance and runs the credit check. |
| ASP (accredited service provider) | A provider approved to send and receive e-invoices over the network on a company's behalf. None is connected yet. |
| Audit log | An append-only list of who changed what in the setup, and when, with the before and after values. |
| Batch | A quantity of one item received together, with its own batch number and expiry date. |
| Chart of accounts | The list of all ledger accounts, arranged in groups. |
| Cost of goods sold (COGS) | The cost of the stock that left on a sale, at the item's weighted average cost on that day. |
| Credit control | The check on approving a sale against the customer's credit limit and overdue invoices. It has three modes: off, warn and block. |
| Credit note | A document that reduces what a customer owes, for example after a price correction. Not to be confused with a sales return, which also moves stock. |
| Debit note | A document that reduces what is owed to a vendor, or raises a charge to a customer, by a price adjustment. |
| Dr / Cr | Debit and credit. Every posting has equal debits and credits. |
| Draft | A saved document that has not been approved. It has no effect on stock or the ledger. |
| e-Invoice | An invoice sent as structured data over an accredited network, in the UAE PINT AE format, rather than as paper or PDF. |
| Exempt | A supply on which no VAT is charged and none can be recovered. It is reported in its own box, apart from zero-rated supplies. |
| FEFO (first expiry, first out) | The rule for issuing stock: the batch that expires first is sold first. A batch the customer's minimum shelf life rules out is skipped. |
| Fiscal year | The accounting year, by default 1 January to 31 December in Dubai time. A closed fiscal year accepts no postings. |
| Go-live | The day the new books start. Opening balances are entered as they stood on that day. |
| GRN (goods received note) | The record of goods received. This system has no separate GRN document: approving a purchase order records the receipt, and the order is flagged as GRN generated. |
| IFRS | International Financial Reporting Standards. The IFRS tab prepares the statements in that layout, with basic notes. |
| KYC (know your customer) | Documents that identify a customer or vendor, such as a trade licence or Emirates ID, with issue and expiry dates. |
| Ledger | The one general record of all debit and credit lines, by account and date. Reports read it. |
| Natural balance | The side on which an account normally carries its balance: debit for assets and expenses, credit for liabilities, equity and income. |
| Number series | The counter that issues document numbers such as SO-2026-0001, one per kind of document and fiscal year. |
| Opening balance equity | The equity account against which all opening balances are posted, so that the opening books balance. Its balance after go-live shows the difference between what was entered as assets and as liabilities. |
| Out of scope | A supply outside the VAT system. It appears in no box of the VAT return. |
| Participant ID | A party's address on the e-invoicing network, in the form scheme:number, for example 0235 followed by a colon and the number. |
| PDC (post-dated cheque) | A cheque dated in the future. It is held in a Cheques in Hand or Cheques Issued account until its date, then cleared to the bank. |
| Period lock | The rule that nothing is posted to a date outside an open fiscal year. |
| Posting | Writing the debit and credit lines of a document to the ledger. |
| Posting map | The list that says which ledger account each kind of entry uses, for example Output VAT or Cost of goods sold. An unmapped entry is refused: there is no fallback account. |
| RCM (reverse charge mechanism) | A treatment where the buyer, not the seller, accounts for the VAT. The VAT return reports it in boxes 3 and 10. The ledger posting of the self-assessment is not built. |
| Reversal | The paired entry that undoes an earlier one. The system reverses rather than erases. |
| Sandbox | A built-in test provider for e-invoicing. It behaves like a service but delivers nothing to a real network. |
| SKU | The stock-keeping unit code a user gives an item. It must be unique. |
| Tax code | A named VAT treatment with a rate, for example Standard 5%, Zero-rated, Exempt, Out of scope or Reverse charge. |
| TRN (tax registration number) | The 15-digit VAT number issued by the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). |
| UOM | Unit of measure, for example kilogram, carton or piece, with conversions between units. |
| VAT 201 | The UAE VAT return form. Its boxes are listed in section 4.11. |
| Voucher | A financial document: receipt, payment, journal, contra (cash and bank transfer), expense, debit note or credit note. |
| Weighted average cost | The cost per unit found by dividing the total cost of stock on hand by its quantity. A purchase changes it; a sale does not. |
| Zero-rated | A supply taxed at 0% in which the seller can still recover input VAT. It is reported in box 4. |
