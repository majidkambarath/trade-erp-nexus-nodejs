# The CRM: enquiries, deals and follow-ups

The CRM covers everything before a customer exists. The rest of Zarvia takes over once they do.

## Lead, deal, customer

- A **lead** is an enquiry from somebody who is not a customer yet.
- **Qualifying** a lead makes them a real customer and opens a **deal** at the same time. Nothing is
  typed twice, and the customer is the same record the rest of Zarvia uses.
- A **deal** is one piece of business: what it is for, what it is worth, and which stage it is on.

## The pipeline moves itself

This is the part worth knowing. You do not drag a deal from stage to stage: your own documents move
it.

- A quotation marked as sent moves the deal to "Quotation sent"
- The customer accepting it moves it to "Offer accepted"
- Approving the sales invoice marks it **won, at the invoice's own value** - not at anybody's guess

If a document is later rejected, cancelled or deleted, the deal goes back to where it was before
that document moved it - never to the beginning. Every automatic move is listed on the deal under
**How it got here**, naming the document that caused it. That is the answer to "it says won and
nobody touched it".

Two cases are left alone on purpose: a purchase document never moves a deal, and neither does an
invoice for a customer who has two open deals - guessing would move the wrong one, so you move it
yourself.

## Nothing is left with nobody owing anything

Every open stage leaves one follow-up, due by that stage's own timing. **Follow-ups** is the
worklist and opens on whatever is late; the Board view shows the same work by day or by person.
Finishing a follow-up asks what came of it and plans the next one in the same breath.

## Where things are

- **Contacts** are people, and a contact of a customer is the same person the customer's own record
  shows - edited in one place, correct in both.
- **Communications** is everything said: calls, visits, messages and notes, newest first. A call is
  logged from the enquiry, deal or person it is about, which is why there is no "log something"
  button here.
- **Calendar** shows follow-ups on the day they are due, deals on their expected close, and
  enquiries on the day they arrived.
- **Setup** is where the stages are, and the stages are the rules: rename one, change its chance of
  closing, or change how soon it asks you to follow up.

## What it will not do

It does not send marketing campaigns, capture enquiries from a website form, or read replies to an
email you sent from it.
