# Party reports

What customers owe and vendors are owed, how late it is, and the history of one party.

| Report | Question it answers | Where to find it | Filters | Export | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Party balances (credit exposure) | What do customers owe and vendors are owed on a date? Credit limit used (No limit set, Within limit, Near limit at 80%, Over limit) and what is overdue. | Reports, Balances /party-balances | As at date; Search; Show (Everyone, Over limit, Near or over limit, With overdue invoices); Include zero balances | CSV | Available |
| Ageing | How late is what customers owe, or we owe vendors? Buckets: Not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, Over 90 days. | Reports, Ageing /ageing | Receivables or Payables; As at date | CSV | Available |
| Account statement | One customer's or vendor's history with a running balance; from the ledger, or from approved documents when posting is off. | Reports, Account statement /statement | Customer or Vendor; party (searchable); From and To | CSV, Print | Available |
