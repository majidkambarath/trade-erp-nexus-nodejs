# Stock reports and batches

What is in stock, what it is worth, how it moves and what is about to expire.

| Report | Question it answers | Where to find it | Filters | Export | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stock valuation | What is in stock and what is it worth? Reconciled to the Inventory account, with differences explained. | Reports, Stock, tab Valuation /stock-reports | As at date; Category; Search; Group by Item or Category | CSV | Available |
| Stock movement | Opening, purchases, returns, sales, write-offs, adjustments and closing per item, by quantity or value. | Reports, Stock, tab Movement | From and To; Category; Search; Show Quantity or Value | CSV | Available |
| Item ledger | Every movement of one item with batch, unit cost and running quantity and value. | Reports, Stock, tab Item ledger | Item (required); From and To | CSV | Available |
| Sales analysis | Revenue, actual cost of goods sold, gross profit and margin by item, category or customer; or purchases by item, category or vendor. | Reports, Stock, tab Sales analysis | From and To; Sales or Purchases; Group by; Category; Search | CSV | Available |
| Expiry | Which batches have expired or expire within N days, in first-expiry-first-out order, and what a write-off would cost? | Reports, Stock, tab Expiry | Expiring within (days, default 30); Category; Search | CSV | Available |
| Slow stock | Which items with stock have not sold for N days? | Reports, Stock, tab Slow stock | No sale in (days, default 90) | CSV | Available |
| Reorder | Which items are at or below reorder level, with shortfall at cost and usual vendor? | Reports, Stock, tab Reorder | Category; Search | CSV | Available |
| Batches and expiry | Every batch in stock with expiry and quantity, and a write-off action. | Inventory, Batches /batches | Show: all, expired, expiring within 7, 30 or 90 days | None | Available |
