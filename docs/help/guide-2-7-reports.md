# Reports

What the books and the stock say, and the VAT return. Section 5 lists each report.

| Screen (tab) | What it is for | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statements | Trial balance, profit and loss, cash flow, balance sheet. | /financial-statements | Available |
| IFRS | Five IFRS statements with comparatives. | /ifrs-statements | Available |
| Ledger | General ledger, day book, journals register, cash and bank book. | /ledger-reports | Available |
| Balances | Party balances and credit exposure, customers and vendors. | /party-balances | Available |
| Ageing | Receivables and payables by days past due. | /ageing | Available |
| Account statement | Statement of account for one customer or vendor with running balance. | /statement | Available |
| Stock | Seven stock reports. | /stock-reports | Available |
| Currency | Foreign-currency receipts and payments with rate and AED value. | /currency-register | Available |
| VAT | VAT return, its documents, saved returns. | /vat-reports | Available |
| e-Invoicing | Dashboard, outbound, inbound, readiness, settings; built-in sandbox only. Marked Soon in the menu. | /e-invoicing | Available (sandbox) |
