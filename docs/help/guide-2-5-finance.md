# Finance

Money in and out of the company, and the manual vouchers. Everything posts to the one chart of accounts.

| Screen (tab) | What it is for | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receipts | Money from customers, set against invoices oldest first, in five payment modes. | /receipt-voucher | Available |
| Payments | Money to vendors, set against purchase orders, in five payment modes. | /payment-voucher | Available |
| Journal | Manual journal with any number of lines that must balance. | /journal-voucher | Available |
| Contra | Transfers between cash and bank accounts. | /contra-voucher | Available |
| Expenses | Expenses with VAT and a payment mode. | /expense-voucher | Available |
| Notes | Debit and credit notes, optionally set against an open invoice. | /debit-credit-notes | Available |
| Cheques | Cheque register with clear, bounce and cancel. | /cheques | Available |
| Cash & bank | Cash and bank balances and cheques to collect. | /cash-and-bank | Available |
| Ledger | Every posting to one chosen account, with running balance in Dr or Cr. | /ledger | Available |
