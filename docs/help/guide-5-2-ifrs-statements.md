# IFRS statements

The same ledger laid out in the IFRS order, with comparatives and basic notes, for the accountant and the auditor.

| Report | Question it answers | Where to find it | Filters | Export | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statement of financial position (IAS 1) | Assets, equity and liabilities, current and non-current, with a comparative. | Reports, IFRS, tab Financial position /ifrs-statements | As at date; Comparative (Prior year, Prior period, None); Show account detail | CSV, Print | Available |
| Statement of profit or loss | Revenue to profit for the period by function. Other comprehensive income is fixed at zero. | Reports, IFRS, tab Profit or loss | From and To; Comparative; Show account detail | CSV, Print | Available |
| Statement of changes in equity | How share capital, retained earnings and reserves moved in the period; checked against the position statement. | Reports, IFRS, tab Changes in equity | From and To; Comparative | CSV, Print | Available |
| Statement of cash flows (IAS 7) | Indirect method: operating, investing, financing; checked against the cash and bank ledger balance. | Reports, IFRS, tab Cash flows | From and To; Comparative | CSV, Print | Available |
| Notes | Basic notes written from the ledger, with tables for receivables and payables (with ageing), inventories, cash and VAT. The screen says they are not a complete set of IFRS disclosures. | Reports, IFRS, tab Notes | As at date; Comparative | CSV, Print | Available |
| Comparatives | The same figures for the prior year (same dates a year earlier) or the prior period (same length just before). | Comparative selector on each IFRS tab | Prior year (default), Prior period, None | In the export | Available |
