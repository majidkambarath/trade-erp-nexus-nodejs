# A worked month

This chapter follows seven documents through one month, September 2026, and then reads every report from the resulting ledger. The dates inside the month are illustrative (the test posts everything on one day); no result depends on them, except the ageing line, which assumes 30-day payment terms and is computed from the rules in section 4.10.

**Setting.** A fresh company with the default chart (section 2.3), posting on, no fiscal year restrictions. Customer Al Noor (credit limit AED 500.00) and vendor Gulf Mills are created in that order, so their main accounts are AR0001 and AP0001. The first receipt then creates Customer Advance - Al Noor (AP0002); the first payment creates Advance to Vendor - Gulf Mills (AR0002); the bank account ENBD Current is added in the Bank group (BANK0002).

**Item.** Rice, bought and sold in bags; the first purchase sets the average cost at AED 10.00.
