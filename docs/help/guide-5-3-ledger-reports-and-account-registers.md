# Ledger reports and account registers

The books in detail, account by account and voucher by voucher, and the registers of cash, bank and cheques.

| Report | Question it answers | Where to find it | Filters | Export | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| General ledger | Opening, movement and closing of every account, by group. | Reports, Ledger, tab General ledger /ledger-reports | From and To; Category; Groups only | CSV | Available |
| Day book | Which vouchers were posted? Open one to see its debit and credit lines. | Reports, Ledger, tab Day book | From and To; Voucher type; Search by number, party or narration | CSV | Available |
| Journals register | Every journal voucher with its lines and whether it balances. | Reports, Ledger, tab Journals | From and To | CSV | Available |
| Cash and bank book | Opening, receipts, payments and closing of each cash and bank account. | Reports, Ledger, tab Cash and bank | From and To; Cash and bank, Cash only, Bank only | CSV | Available |
| Account ledger | Every posting to one account with a running balance, as Dr or Cr. | Finance, Ledger /ledger | Account (searchable); optional From and To | Print | Available |
| Cash and bank balances | Balance of each cash and bank account, and cheques to collect. | Finance, Cash & bank /cash-and-bank | None | None | Available |
| Cheque register | Which cheques are pending, cleared, bounced or cancelled? | Finance, Cheques /cheques | Status tab; Direction; Search | None | Available |
