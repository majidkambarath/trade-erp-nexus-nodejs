# Available now

Grouped by area. A capability is listed as available when it is built, reachable from the menu and working on live data.

| Area | Capability | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Trade | Purchase orders with approval, receipt into stock, batch and expiry, and vendor payable; sales orders and tax invoices with server pricing, tax codes, charges, credit control (off, warn, block), first-expiry-first-out issue and cost of goods sold | Available |
| Trade | Sales and purchase returns linked to the original, with quantity and value limits, a return window and a require-link switch; attachments and PDF on orders; numbering by the server | Available |
| Parties | Customer and vendor master (VAT and TRN, credit limit, terms, contacts, bank accounts, KYC documents with expiry status); a ledger account for every party | Available |
| Stock | Items, categories, units, batches with expiry, weighted average cost with a movement audit, recost of back-dated documents, write-off of expired or damaged batches posted to the ledger | Available |
| Stock | Manual stock adjustment: costed (out at the average cost, in at the cost typed or the average), batches first-expiry-first-out, one movement, numbered SA-, posted Dr Inventory / Cr Stock adjustment for a gain and the reverse for a loss, refused in a closed fiscal year | Available |
| Finance | Receipts and payments with oldest-first allocation, advances and five payment modes; cheque register; card fees and credit-card limit; bank, card-type and card masters | Available |
| Finance | Journals, contra, expenses with VAT, debit and credit notes | Available |
| Finance | Currency master with rates and tolerance; foreign-currency receipts and payments; currency register | Available |
| Accounts | Chart of accounts with a default chart; posting map with no fallback accounts; ledger posting switch; fiscal years with period lock; tax codes with rate history; audit log | Available |
| Accounts | Opening balances for go-live: accounts, customer and vendor open invoices, opening stock, review | Available |
| Reports | Trial balance, profit and loss, cash flow, balance sheet; IFRS statements with comparatives and notes; general ledger, day book, journals, cash and bank book, account ledger | Available |
| Reports | Party balances, ageing, account statement; seven stock reports; VAT return (boxes 1a to 14 except 2, 6 and 7) with documents and saved returns | Available |
| Reports | e-Invoicing: readiness, preview, send, status, retry, inbound register, settings, against the built-in sandbox | Available (sandbox) |
| Settings | Company profile, business rules, tax identity, invoice bank details, display preferences, password change | Available |
| Shell | Labelled rail, header tabs, Ctrl+K page search, mobile drawer, dark mode, date and time formats in Dubai time | Available |
| Home | Dashboard with real figures in all four tabs | Available |
