# Accounts

The ledger itself: what accounts exist, how they are grouped, how entries reach them, and how the books are set up.

| Screen (tab) | What it is for | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chart of accounts | The chart: five categories, groups and accounts, with balances, attachments and a ledger window. | /chart-of-accounts | Available |
| Banks | Bank master, used by accounts, parties and cheques. | /banks | Available |
| KYC documents | Expiring documents of customers and vendors, and the KYC document types. | /kyc-documents | Available |
| Card types | Card types such as Visa, with a processing-fee percentage. | /card-types | Available |
| Cards | Terminal, credit, debit and prepaid cards, with settlement bank and credit limit. | /cards | Available |
| Opening balances | Go-live set-up in six steps: date, accounts, customers, vendors, stock, review. | /opening-balances | Available |
| Currencies | Currency master and exchange-rate history. AED is the read-only base. | /currencies | Available |
| Setup | Posting accounts and the posting switch, Fiscal years and numbering, Tax codes, Audit log. | /accounting-setup | Available |
