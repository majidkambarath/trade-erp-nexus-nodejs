# Sales

Everything that happens between the company and its customers, from the order to the money owed.

| Screen (tab) | What it is for | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | Sales orders that become tax invoices when approved: server pricing, credit control, first-expiry-first-out issue, bulk actions, PDF copies. | /sales-order | Available |
| Returns | Sales returns against an approved invoice, limited by quantity and value. | /sales-return | Available |
| Customers | Customer master: TRN, credit limit, terms, contacts, banks, KYC documents. | /customer-creation | Available |
| Receivables | Customer accounts: balance, credit used, overdue, statement, open invoices. | /credit-accounts | Available |
