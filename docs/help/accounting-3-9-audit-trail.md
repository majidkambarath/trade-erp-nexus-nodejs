# The audit trail: who did what to a document

Every invoice, return and voucher in Zarvia keeps its own audit trail. It shows who saved it, edited it, approved, rejected, cancelled or deleted it, and when. It also shows what the document did to your books.

## Where to open it

The audit trail sits on the document itself. It is not a separate report.

- **A payment, a receipt or any other voucher:** open the voucher from its list (Payments, Receipts, Journal, Contra, Expenses, Debit and credit notes), then press **Audit trail** at the bottom of the voucher.
- **A sales or purchase invoice, or a return:** press **Audit trail** on the row or card in the list, or on the document's own screen.
- **A cheque:** press **Audit trail** beside it in the cheque register.
- **From the ledger:** in an account's ledger, press the voucher number on a line to open the voucher behind that posting, then press **Audit trail**.

## What it shows

1. **Activity:** each action, with who did it and when.
2. **Ledger entries:** the debits and credits the document posted, and any reversal of them.
3. For a payment or a receipt, **the invoices it settled** and anything left **on account**.
4. For an invoice, **the stock it moved**, **the payments that settled it**, and anything sent to the customer.

## The whole organisation's log

To see every action across the organisation rather than one document's, open **Accounting setup** and choose the **Audit log** tab. It is newest first, and you need the audit permission to see it.
