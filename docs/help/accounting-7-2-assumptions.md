# Assumptions

**One company, one currency ledger.** A single default company and branch; the ledger is in AED. A second company or base currency has not been exercised.

**Dates.** A ledger entry is dated the document or voucher date; a document entered with a plain date is stored at 04:00 Dubai time, so it belongs to the day typed. The fiscal year is the calendar year by default and number series restart each fiscal year.

**Costing and VAT.** Weighted-average cost is the only method. The standard VAT rate is 5 percent, held in tax codes with a rate history. The accountant owns the mapping: a default account named in this document stands for whichever account the key is mapped to.
