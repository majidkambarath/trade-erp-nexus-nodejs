# Tab 3: Inventory

Where the stock value sits and which items or batches need action.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Category cards | The four largest categories by stock value, with item count and share. |
| Inventory mix | Stock value by category as a donut of the top five and Others. |
| Stock alerts | Up to six items at or below reorder level and up to six batches expired or expiring within 30 days, in first-expiry-first-out order. |
| Stock value trend | Month-end balance of the Inventory account for 8 months. |
