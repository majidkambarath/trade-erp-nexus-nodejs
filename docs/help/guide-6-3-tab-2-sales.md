# Tab 2: Sales

Orders, average value and the best sellers and customers.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Orders | Number of approved sales invoices in the period, with the change on the period before. |
| Average order | Net value before VAT of an approved invoice. |
| Orders approved | Approved sales orders as a percentage of all sales orders in the period, with the change in points. |
| Sales vs purchase | Monthly sales (ledger) and purchases (documents) for 8 months. |
| Best sellers | The four best items by net revenue, each with its change. |
| Daily order trend | Approved sales orders for each of the last 7 days. |
| Sales by customer | The five largest customers by net revenue, with a link to party balances. |
