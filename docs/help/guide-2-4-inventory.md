# Inventory

The stock master, the movement history and the batches with expiry dates.

| Screen (tab) | What it is for | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock Items | Item master and detail: SKU, category, unit, reorder level, prices, stock, purchase history. | /stock-item-creation | Available |
| Movements | Every stock movement, with filters and CSV, and a Record Movement form (costed and posted, see 4.7). | /inventory | Available |
| Categories | Item categories. | /category-management | Available |
| Units | Units of measure and conversions (stored, not yet used by orders or stock). | /unit-setup | Available |
| Batches | Batches with expiry and quantity on hand; write-off of expired or damaged stock. | /batches | Available |
