# Tab 1: Dashboard

The first tab answers: how is the business doing this period, and what needs me now?

| Widget | What it shows | Where the figure comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Sales value | Revenue for the period with a change on the previous period, an 8-month chart and the peak month. | Profit and loss revenue from the ledger |
| Average margin | Gross profit as a percentage of revenue. | Profit and loss from the ledger |
| Average order | Net value of an approved sales invoice before VAT. | Approved sales invoices |
| Collection rate | Approved receipts divided by VAT-inclusive invoices in the period. It can exceed 100%. | Receipt vouchers and approved sales invoices |
| Operations status | Active orders, pending purchase orders, low-stock items and new customers, each with a count and a total. | Approved and draft documents; reorder rule; customers |
| Top product | Best item by net revenue, with its change and a 7-month trend. | Stock sales analysis (VAT-excluded, net of returns) |
| VAT this quarter | Output VAT, input VAT and the net, labelled Payable or Refundable, with a count of lines that have no tax treatment. No due date is shown, because the system does not record whether the company files monthly or quarterly. | The VAT return for the quarter so far, and the Output VAT account |
| Needs attention | Links to: sales and purchase orders waiting for approval, VAT lines with no treatment, stock value that does not agree with the Inventory account, customers over their credit limit, batches expired, batches expiring within 30 days. Each appears only when it is above zero. | Documents, VAT return, stock reconciliation, party balances, batches |
| Recent activity | The 8 newest ledger vouchers with a Paid, Part-paid, Unpaid or Posted label. It is not tied to the chosen period. | Day book |
| Quick actions | Links to Sales Order, Purchase Order, Stock Items and VAT Reports. | Navigation only |
| Charts below | Weekly orders against returns; customer mix; performance rings; sales, purchases and profit by month; category sales and radar; cash inflow against outflow; open orders; receipts and payments this week; order pipeline; invoice settlement; top customers and vendors; ageing; collections against invoicing; item revenue map; documents by hour. | Ledger, documents, ageing and stock records |

| Note. The "Sales vs purchase vs profit" chart mixes two sources: sales and gross profit come from the ledger, purchases come from documents (before VAT, net of returns). The "Orders" figure counts approved sales invoices. The category sales chart always compares the calendar month of the period end with the month before it. |
|---|
