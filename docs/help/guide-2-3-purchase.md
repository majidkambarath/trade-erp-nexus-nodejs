# Purchase

Everything that happens between the company and its vendors, from the order to the money owed.

| Screen (tab) | What it is for | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | Purchase orders. Approving one receives goods into stock with batch and expiry and raises the payable. | /purchase-order | Available |
| Returns | Purchase returns against an approved purchase order. | /purchase-return | Available |
| Vendors | Vendor master (same six tabs as customers, no credit limit). | /vendor-creation | Available |
| Payables | Vendor accounts: balance owed, overdue, statement, open invoices. | /debit-accounts | Available |
