# Profit and loss with gross profit

Table 58. Profit and loss, September 2026 (test: revenue 200, direct costs 100, gross profit 100, margin 50, operating expenses 200, net profit -100)

| Line | Accounts | Amount (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sales Revenue (credit 200.00) | 200.00 |
| Direct costs | Cost of Goods Sold (debit 100.00: 10 bags at 10.00) | 100.00 |
| Gross profit | Revenue - Direct costs; gross margin 100.00 / 200.00 = 50.00 percent | 100.00 |
| Other income | none | 0.00 |
| Operating expenses | Rent Expense (debit 200.00) | 200.00 |
| Net profit | Gross profit + Other income - Operating expenses = 100.00 + 0.00 - 200.00 | -100.00 |

The input VAT on the rent (10.00) is not an expense: it went to Input VAT. The loss of 100.00 comes from the rent being larger than the margin on one small sale.
