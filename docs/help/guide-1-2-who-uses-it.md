# Who uses it

The menu is arranged by business job. The names below are the role names used in the menu. Section 3 explains what the server enforces today, which is less than the menu suggests.

| Role (menu name) | Typical work | Main modules |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / Admin | Sets the company up, approves, reads every report, decides the business rules. | All modules, Settings |
| Accountant | Receipts, payments, journals, expenses, VAT return, period close, statements and ageing. | Finance, Accounts, Reports |
| Sales Executive | Customers, sales orders and sales returns. | Sales |
| Purchase Officer | Vendors, purchase orders and purchase returns. | Purchase |
| Inventory Manager | Items, units, categories, batches, expiry and write-offs, stock reports. | Inventory |
| HR | Staff records. | People |
