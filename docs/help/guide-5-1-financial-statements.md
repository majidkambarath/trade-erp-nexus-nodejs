# Financial statements

The four statements that say how the company has done and where it stands, read from the general ledger.

| Report | Question it answers | Where to find it | Filters | Export | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trial balance | Do debits equal credits, and what is each account's opening, movement and closing position? | Reports, Statements, tab Trial balance /financial-statements | From and To; presets This month, Last month, This year | CSV | Available |
| Profit and loss | What did we earn and spend? Revenue less cost of sales gives gross profit and margin; then net profit or loss. | Reports, Statements, tab Profit and loss | From and To; presets | CSV | Available |
| Cash flow | Where did cash and bank money come from and go, by type of voucher? Does it agree with the cash and bank ledgers? | Reports, Statements, tab Cash flow | From and To; presets | CSV | Available |
| Balance sheet | What do we own, owe and hold in equity on a date? Profit to date sits inside equity. | Reports, Statements, tab Balance sheet | One As at date | None | Available |
