# Auditor guide

The guide for auditors is written once, in the words a client and an auditor read, and served in two places:

- in the app, under **Help** > "A guide for auditors" (and Zarvia AI answers from it);
- as a file: [`docs/help/extra-auditor-guide.md`](help/extra-auditor-guide.md). Print it or send it as it is.

It covers giving an auditor read-only access, the audit log (who, when, from where, each field's old and new
value; it cannot be changed), why nothing posted is ever deleted (approved documents are cancelled and kept,
deleted drafts are kept whole), numbering and its gap report, closed months, years and VAT periods, the VAT
return, its saved copies and PDF, the totals by tax code and emirate, the FTA audit file (FAF), what tax
invoices, credit notes and self-billed invoices carry, how to prove the books are complete, record keeping and
versions.

Edit the help topic, not this file.
